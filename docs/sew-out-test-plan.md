# Sew-out test plan

Real stitch-outs are the only way to tune Ecko's settings. Run these tests
and write down what you see. Each problem maps to a setting we can adjust.

## Tests

| # | Design | Settings | What it checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A simple two-color logo, about 3" wide | Your fabric | Basic quality, gaps between colors |
| 2 | The same logo at 1.5" wide | Same | Small detail and density build-up |
| 3 | Lettering only: "Ecko 123" in Block, 0.5" tall | Woven | Satin quality and letter joints |
| 4 | Lettering only: a name in Script, 0.75" tall | T-shirt | Connected script on knit |
| 5 | A large filled shape (circle or square), 3" | T-shirt | Puckering, fill coverage, underlay |
| 6 | Any logo | Caps (if you do hats) | Centre-out order, registration on the cap frame |

Use a fresh needle, the stabilizer the app recommends, and test on scrap of
the real fabric.

## What to record

For each test, note your machine, fabric, stabilizer, and anything you see
from this list:

- **Gaps between colors or along outlines.** Pull compensation or overlap
  is too small for that fabric.
- **Puckering or wavy fabric.** Density or underlay is too heavy, or the
  stabilizer is too light.
- **Fabric showing through fills.** Density is too low.
- **Thread breaks or bird nests, and where they happen.** Density
  hotspots, or very short stitches.
- **Satin looks loose or loopy.** Satin is too wide for the fabric, or
  needs more underlay.
- **Letters look thin, blobby, or the joints are messy.** Lettering or
  junction handling needs work.
- **Too many trims or jump threads left behind.** Sequencing needs work.
- **Design is off-center or the wrong size.** File or format problem.

Photos of the front and back of each sew-out are the most useful thing to
send back. Note the design name or link from "My designs" with each one,
so the exact file can be reopened.
