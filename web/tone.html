Vintage & bootleg

Keyline and shadow wrap the whole design in one ink, like a 90s bootleg. Ghost is a faint, broken second print offset from the first, like a screen that slipped. For worn-out ink, try Washed out, Fabric weave, Squeegee streaks and Screen pinholes in Texture, or the Bootleg, Flea-market, Sun-faded and Thrashed looks.
